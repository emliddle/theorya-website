Organizational Agency Index

The way you divide work between people and AI today shapes what your organization will be capable of tomorrow.

The assumptions built into that design can expand what's possible for people and AI, or keep you operating within the limits of how work has always been done.

The Organizational Agency Index looks at one AI use case to examine the nature of the work, the roles people and AI play, and the assumptions shaping its design.

Begin the Index

What to expect

The Index takes about 15 minutes and is a guided conversation.

You'll move through a combination of simple choices and conversational questions about one AI use case. Answer based on what you know, and add context when it's helpful.

What you'll receive

At the end, you'll receive an immediate summary and observations based on your responses.

If you'd like to go deeper, you'll have the option to provide your email and receive a more detailed, shareable report.

Before you begin

You don't need to be an AI expert. Your use case can be something you're exploring, piloting or already using. You just need to know it well enough to describe how it works.

You won't need to share proprietary or confidential information. Answer based on what you know about the use case.

You can remain anonymous. Your name and contact information are not required to complete the Index or see your summary.

The Organizational Agency Study

Your responses contribute to the Organizational Agency Study, TheoryA.ai's ongoing research into how organizations are redesigning work and the roles of people and AI.

Individual respondents and organizations are never identified in published research.

Have your use case in mind? You're ready to begin.

The Index reads one use case. The Diagnostic translates what it finds into a decision: what can evolve, what has to be rebuilt, and where to act first.
